Common Questions

Jersey Shore real estate, answered.

The questions buyers and sellers ask us most often.

Is the Jersey Shore a good place to live year round?

Yes. Communities like Stafford Township, Toms River and Barnegat are thriving year round towns with excellent schools, restaurants and amenities beyond the summer season.

How far is Ocean County from Staten Island?

Most Ocean County towns are 45 minutes to 1 hour from Staten Island via the Garden State Parkway.

What is the median home price on LBI in 2026?

The median home price on Long Beach Island is approximately $2,000,000 in Spring 2026, up 11.4% year over year.

What are the best towns in Ocean County for families?

Stafford Township, Toms River and Barnegat consistently rank among the best family friendly towns in Ocean County for schools, safety and community.

How much are property taxes in Ocean County NJ?

Property taxes in Ocean County are significantly lower than North Jersey. Stafford Township averages around $6,000 to $8,000 annually depending on home value.

Is now a good time to sell in Ocean County?

Yes. Inventory is historically low, prices are up 11% year over year and qualified buyers are actively searching. Spring 2026 is a strong seller's market.
