# Lead Generation Engine

The conversion blueprint for Jersey Shore Home Info. This document defines how every page on the site turns a visitor into a qualified lead for Matt Dunn, REALTOR (Weichert Realtors, Ship Bottom, NJ). It is the single source of truth for calls to action, conversion paths, trust building, and lead capture. Page builders should treat this as a required reference alongside PAGE_STANDARDS.md, GOLD_STANDARD_PAGE.md, and the Town Engine documents. Pair it with PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md, which sequences the work that builds and strengthens this engine over time.

## 1. Purpose

The site exists to generate qualified real estate leads while building durable local authority. Authority earns the traffic; the Lead Generation Engine converts that traffic into conversations with Matt. Every page is therefore both a content asset and a conversion surface. No page is "done" until it has a clear, honest path to becoming a lead, and no page should ever leave a motivated visitor without an obvious next step.

This engine is intentionally advisory and low pressure, consistent with the brand. The goal is not to trick visitors into a form; it is to make the most helpful next step the most obvious step, whether that is a phone call, an email, a consultation, or a valuation request.

## 2. Business Goals

The engine supports four business goals, in priority order. First, lead generation: capture buyer, seller, relocation, and high-value vertical inquiries and route them to Matt. Second, brand authority: reinforce deep local expertise so the site is trusted before contact is ever made. Third, organic discoverability: structure content and CTAs so they support SEO and AEO rather than competing with them. Fourth, efficiency: reuse a consistent conversion pattern across pages so new pages convert without bespoke work.

Lead quality matters as much as lead volume. A smaller number of well-qualified, intent-rich conversations is worth more to a single agent than a flood of low-intent form fills. The engine favors capture points that signal real intent (valuation requests, consultation bookings, journey-specific inquiries) over generic newsletter-style sign-ups.

## 3. Conversion Journeys

Each visitor type follows a journey from discovery to a qualified conversation. Every journey shares the same spine: establish relevance, build trust, present the right next step, and capture the lead with minimal friction. The journeys below define the intent, the on-site path, and the ideal capture point for each audience.

### Buyer Journey

Buyers arrive through town guides, the Discovery Center, and relocation content. They are choosing where and what to buy before they are ready to transact. The path moves from town and area discovery, to property goal selection, to the buyer path, and finally to a buyer consultation or direct contact. The ideal capture point is "Schedule a Buyer Consultation" or "Talk With Matt," positioned after the visitor has seen enough local detail to trust the source. Secondary capture is a "what fits my goals" conversation, since live listings now route to Matt directly rather than an MLS feed.

### Seller Journey

Sellers arrive through town guides and selling content and want to understand value and process. The path moves from local market context, to the seller path, to a valuation request or listing consultation. The ideal capture point is "Free Home Valuation," reinforced by a softer "Discuss Selling Your Home" conversation for sellers who are not ready to request a number. Sellers convert on confidence in pricing and local results, so trust triggers (local track record, market knowledge) matter most here.

### Commercial Journey

Commercial prospects are investors and business owners seeking retail, office, mixed-use, or commercial opportunities. Dedicated commercial content is not yet built, so the current path routes these visitors directly to a conversation with Matt rather than a dead end. The ideal capture point is a commercial-specific "Talk With Matt" inquiry that records the asset type and intent. As commercial pages are built (see PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md), this journey gains its own discovery and qualification layer.

### Land Journey

Land prospects seek buildable lots, teardowns, and development parcels, often on or near LBI. Like commercial, dedicated land content is in development, so the path routes to a direct conversation that captures lot criteria (location, buildability, budget). The ideal capture point is a land-specific inquiry. Land buyers value zoning, flood, and feasibility knowledge, so trust triggers should emphasize local technical fluency.

### Investment Journey

Investors evaluate rental yield, seasonal income, and appreciation potential across the shore market. The path connects market context and high-value verticals to an investment-focused conversation with Matt. The ideal capture point is an investment inquiry that captures strategy (short-term rental, long-term hold, value-add). Investors respond to data and candor, so measurement-oriented trust signals work best.

### Luxury Journey

Luxury buyers and sellers expect discretion, curation, and white-glove service for high-value oceanfront and premium properties. The path emphasizes positioning, presentation, and a private conversation rather than mass-market CTAs. The ideal capture point is a discreet "private consultation" inquiry. Luxury conversion depends heavily on presentation quality, so photography and video work in PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md directly enable this journey.

### Relocation Journey

Relocation prospects, notably buyers moving from the NYC metro and Staten Island, need orientation before they can choose a town. The relocation guide is the anchor asset, and the path moves from relocation content, to town and area discovery, to a relocation-focused conversation. The ideal capture point is "Moving From Staten Island?" or a relocation consultation. This is one of the highest-intent audiences and deserves dedicated, origin-specific content over time.

## 4. Calls to Action

### Primary CTAs

Primary CTAs are the highest-intent actions and should be visible without scrolling on key pages and repeated at natural decision points. The primary set is: Free Home Valuation (seller intent), Schedule a Buyer Consultation (buyer intent), and Talk With Matt (universal direct contact). On any given page, one primary CTA should be dominant and matched to the page's audience: valuation on seller-leaning pages, consultation on buyer-leaning pages, and direct contact everywhere as a fallback. Primary CTAs use the site's prominent button styling and clear, benefit-led labels.

### Secondary CTAs

Secondary CTAs keep lower-intent visitors moving toward a primary action instead of leaving. The secondary set includes: Explore Ocean County Towns, Explore Long Beach Island, Read the Relocation Guide, Browse by Property Goal, Request a Local Market Read, and the buyer and seller path entries. Secondary CTAs are presented as discovery cards and inline links and should always lead somewhere real (an existing page or anchor) so no path becomes a dead end.

## 5. Channel Strategies

### Phone Strategy

The phone is the fastest, highest-intent capture channel for a single agent. The office and mobile number should be tappable (tel: links) on every page, visible in the header or hero area, and present in the footer. Phone CTAs should read as an invitation to a no-pressure conversation rather than a sales hotline. Where possible, future call tracking should attribute calls to the originating page and journey so phone leads are measured alongside form leads.

### Email Strategy

Email serves visitors who prefer asynchronous contact or have detailed questions. A tappable mailto: link should be available site-wide, and journey-specific inquiry paths should pre-frame the message subject (for example, buyer consultation, valuation, relocation) so Matt can triage quickly. Email capture should never feel like signing up for a list; it is a direct line to the agent. A future contact form (see PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md) will structure these inquiries and feed a CRM.

### Consultation Strategy

Consultations are the core qualified-lead product: a scheduled, structured conversation with Matt. Buyer consultations, seller consultations, and private luxury consultations each capture the visitor's goals, timeline, and constraints. Today these route through direct contact; the planned upgrade is a scheduling flow that books time directly and reduces back-and-forth. Consultation CTAs should appear after the visitor has consumed enough value to be ready to commit a conversation.

### Valuation Strategy

The Free Home Valuation is the flagship seller capture. It offers clear value (what is my home worth) in exchange for contact and property details, and it signals genuine selling intent. The valuation CTA should be the dominant action on seller-leaning pages and consistently available in navigation. Honesty is non-negotiable per NON_NEGOTIABLES.md: valuations are an invitation to a real, expert assessment, not an automated guarantee, and no fabricated numbers are ever shown.

## 6. Trust Triggers

Conversion depends on trust earned before the ask. The core trust triggers are: visible brokerage and licensing information (Weichert Realtors, NJ License #2442118), the Fair Housing and Equal Housing Opportunity commitment, the honest-data disclaimer, a real agent identity (headshot and bio), demonstrated local expertise in the content itself, and social proof through testimonials and results once available. Several of these are present today; the missing assets (real headshot, brokerage logos, credentials, testimonials) are tracked as high-priority backlog items because they lift conversion across every journey simultaneously. Trust triggers should sit near primary CTAs so credibility and the ask reinforce each other.

## 7. Lead Magnet Opportunities

Lead magnets exchange genuine value for contact and intent signals. The highest-fit opportunities for this site are: town-level market reports (request a local market read), neighborhood and town comparison guides, an origin-specific relocation guide (expanding beyond Staten Island), buyer and seller checklists, a flood and insurance primer for shore properties, and a seasonal market outlook. Each magnet should map to a journey and capture an intent signal, not just an email. Magnets must respect the honest-data rule: any figures must be verified, sourced, and dated, or framed as an expert conversation rather than a precise claim.

## 8. Future Capabilities

### Future CRM Integration

As inquiry volume grows, leads should flow into a CRM rather than only an inbox. The CRM should capture source page, journey, and intent, support follow-up sequences, and prevent leads from slipping through. Integration will likely require Netlify environment variables and a form backend; per the infrastructure policy, sensitive variables on untrusted deploys require approval. CRM work is sequenced in PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md after the contact form and analytics foundation exist.

### Future AI Qualification

An AI assistant can qualify leads before they reach Matt by answering common town and process questions, capturing goals and timelines conversationally, and routing high-intent visitors to the right CTA. Done well, AI qualification raises lead quality and reduces low-value back-and-forth. It depends on accurate, verified content (the Town Engine and verified statistics) so the assistant never fabricates local data. This is a later-phase capability tracked in the backlog.

## 9. Measurement

### Success Metrics

The engine is measured by the conversations it produces and their quality. Core metrics: total qualified leads (calls, emails, consultations, valuations), lead-to-conversation rate, conversion rate by page and by journey, valuation requests, consultation bookings, and the assisted contribution of town pages and the relocation guide. Supporting metrics: organic traffic and rankings (authority working), CTA click-through, and form completion rate (friction). Each metric should tie back to a journey so it is clear which audience and which page are converting.

Instrumentation is a prerequisite, not an afterthought. The site currently lacks a formal analytics and lead-tracking layer; standing one up is an early backlog item because nothing in this engine can be optimized until it can be measured. Until then, success is tracked qualitatively through inbound contact volume and quality.

## 10. Future Expansion

The engine grows along three axes. Coverage: extend dedicated, converting content to every journey, including commercial, land, investment, and luxury verticals and additional relocation origins, so no audience routes to a generic fallback. Depth: add lead magnets, scheduling, and richer trust assets (photography, video, testimonials, market reports) that lift conversion across journeys. Intelligence: layer analytics, CRM, and AI qualification so leads are measured, nurtured, and pre-qualified automatically. Every expansion item lives in PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md, ranked by ROI, so growth is driven by business impact rather than guesswork.
