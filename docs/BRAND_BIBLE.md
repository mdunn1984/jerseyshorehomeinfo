# Brand Bible — Jersey Shore Home Info

The identity, voice, and positioning guide for Jersey Shore Home Info. Use this to keep every page, headline, and piece of copy sounding like one consistent, trustworthy local expert.

## 1. Brand essence

Jersey Shore Home Info is the local-authority real estate resource for Ocean County and Long Beach Island. It is the digital presence of Matt Dunn, REALTOR (Weichert Realtors), positioned as a knowledgeable, low-pressure local guide rather than a high-volume sales machine.

## 2. Positioning

- Hyper-local expertise: deep, town-by-town knowledge of LBI and mainland Ocean County.
- Trusted advisor: helpful first, transactional second. "No-pressure conversation about your next move."
- Long-term resource: a reference buyers and sellers return to, not a one-time landing page.

## 3. Voice and tone

- Knowledgeable but approachable. Sounds like a seasoned local who genuinely knows the towns.
- Honest and grounded. Real numbers, real tradeoffs (price points, buyer pools, commute math).
- Calm and confident. No hype, no pressure, no clickbait.
- Warm and welcoming, especially toward relocating buyers (for example the Staten Island relocation guide).

## 4. Audience

- Buyers relocating to or within the Jersey Shore (including from Staten Island and the NYC metro).
- Sellers seeking a local agent with market knowledge.
- Second-home and shore-property buyers along LBI.

## 5. Naming and references

- Brand name: Jersey Shore Home Info (stylized on-site as "Jersey ShoreHomeInfo").
- Always pair the agent with the brokerage: Matt Dunn, REALTOR, Weichert Realtors.
- Include required compliance references where appropriate: NJ License #2442118, Equal Housing Opportunity, Fair Housing.

## 6. Visual identity (to be formalized)

- Aesthetic: clean, coastal, professional. Aerials, waterfronts, downtowns, and real homes.
- Photography: authentic local imagery preferred over generic stock. (Open blocker: town pages currently show photo placeholders.)
- Trust assets to add: agent headshot, Weichert branding, credentials, and client reviews.

## 7. Brand do and do-not

- Do: lead with local knowledge, be specific, be helpful, invite a relaxed conversation.
- Do: keep typography and punctuation clean (avoid the mojibake characters currently appearing in some copy).
- Do not: use pressure tactics, exaggerated claims, or generic filler.
- Do not: misrepresent listing data; always note information is believed accurate but not guaranteed.
