# Business Plan — Jersey Shore Home Info

The business context behind the website. This document explains who the business serves, how the website supports it, and what success looks like. It is a working plan, not a legal or financial document, and should be refined as the business evolves.

## 1. Overview

Jersey Shore Home Info is the lead-generation and authority-building platform for Matt Dunn, REALTOR (Weichert Realtors, Ship Bottom, NJ). The business is residential real estate brokerage services across Ocean County and Long Beach Island, with the website serving as the primary digital storefront and local-expertise hub.

## 2. Value proposition

- Deep local knowledge of LBI and mainland Ocean County towns.
- A no-pressure, advisory approach for buyers and sellers.
- A single, trustworthy online resource for town information, listings, and home valuations.

## 3. Target customers

- Buyers relocating from the NYC metro (notably Staten Island) to the Jersey Shore.
- Local move-up and move-down buyers within Ocean County.
- Sellers wanting a knowledgeable local listing agent.
- Second-home and shore-property buyers on LBI.

## 4. How the website supports the business

- Authority: town guides establish local expertise and earn organic search traffic (supported by the SEO/AEO pass).
- Discovery: FBS SmartFrame IDX provides live MLS search and office listings.
- Conversion: clear calls to action for home valuations and direct contact with Matt Dunn.
- Trust: legal pages, fair-housing commitment, and (planned) credentials and reviews.

## 5. Lead generation model

- Primary CTAs: "View Homes For Sale," "Free Home Valuation," and direct contact.
- Channels: organic search (SEO/AEO), direct/referral, and brokerage presence.
- Capture points: valuation requests and contact inquiries route leads to Matt Dunn.

## 6. Revenue

Revenue is earned through real estate commissions on closed buyer and seller transactions sourced or supported by the website. The site itself is a marketing and lead asset, not a direct revenue product.

## 7. Competitive positioning

- Versus large portals: more local depth, a named local expert, and a relationship-first tone.
- Versus generic agent sites: richer town-level content and a genuine resource for the whole shore.

## 8. Success metrics (to define and track)

- Organic search visibility for target towns.
- Valuation and contact lead volume.
- Lead-to-appointment and appointment-to-closing conversion.
- Returning visitors and engagement on town pages.

Complete Phase 1 launch readiness (real photos, encoding fixes, trust assets) so the site fully supports lead generation. See ROADMAP.md.
