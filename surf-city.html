Neighborhood Overview

About Surf City

Surf City sits at the geographic center of Long Beach Island, just north of Ship Bottom — an equidistant 10-mile drive to either end of LBI. Compact, walkable and busy year-round, Surf City has carved out an identity as the island's most concentrated downtown experience outside of Beach Haven.

The Long Beach Boulevard strip through Surf City is packed with independent retail — surf shops, art galleries, surf-and-skate brands, ice cream, coffee houses, restaurants and the legendary How You Brewin' coffee. The borough's commercial vibe is younger and more creative than the rest of LBI, drawing a steady mix of artists, surfers and second-home families.

Surf City lives up to its name. The beaches between 5th and 16th Street are among the most popular surfing breaks on LBI, and the borough hosts annual surf competitions that bring the East Coast surf community to town every summer. Several surf shops along the boulevard outfit locals and visitors year-round.

The housing stock is a balanced mix of older shore cottages, mid-century cape cods and a steady stream of new construction. Ocean blocks command the premium, but Surf City still offers more attainable price points than Beach Haven or Long Beach Township, with bay-side homes occasionally listing under $1M.

The year-round community here is notably tighter than other LBI boroughs. Walkable scale, the boulevard's everyday foot traffic and a critical mass of small businesses keep Surf City feeling like a true town rather than a seasonal stop. Families and remote workers have increasingly chosen Surf City as a primary residence.

Rental demand stays strong all summer thanks to the central location — guests can hit Barnegat Light to the north or Beach Haven to the south in under 15 minutes. Investors love Surf City for the combination of affordability, walkability and rental velocity.

Real Estate Market

Surf City Home Prices & Trends

Surf City offers one of the more balanced markets on LBI — older cottages still trading under $1.5M, mid-century homes in the $1.5M–$2.5M range, and new construction pushing $3M and beyond. The borough's central location and walkable downtown create steady appreciation across all price tiers.

Flood Zone & Insurance

Flood Zone Information

Surf City sits entirely on the barrier island within FEMA AE flood zones, with base flood elevations typically 8–10 feet. Newer construction is elevated to current code, and flood insurance is required for any home with a federally-backed mortgage. Many of the borough's mid-century cottages have been raised in recent years to take advantage of lower insurance premiums and protect against future storms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Surf City NJ Real Estate FAQ

What is Surf City NJ known for?
Surf City is known as the center of Long Beach Island — home to a busy downtown of independent shops, galleries, surf shops, restaurants and some of the best surfing breaks on the Jersey Shore.
What is the median home price in Surf City NJ?
The median home price in Surf City is approximately $1,600,000 in 2026. Bay-side homes can be found from about $1M, while oceanfront and new construction range from $2.5M to $5M.
Is Surf City NJ good for surfing?
Yes. Surf City has some of the most consistent surf breaks on LBI, particularly between 5th and 16th Streets. The borough hosts annual surf competitions and is home to several long-running surf shops including Surf City 5.
What restaurants and shops are in Surf City NJ?
Surf City has one of the most concentrated downtown strips on LBI including How You Brewin' Coffee, Scojo's, The Daymark, Surf City Hotel, multiple surf shops, art galleries and seasonal boutiques.
Is Surf City NJ good for families?
Yes. Surf City is family-friendly with lifeguarded beaches, walkable downtown, central location for exploring LBI, and a community feel that appeals to year-round and seasonal families alike.
What is the rental market like in Surf City NJ?
Surf City has a strong vacation rental market driven by its central location and walkable downtown. Weekly summer rates typically range from $4,500 for cottages to $12,000+ for newer ocean-block homes.
How far is Surf City from the LBI bridge?
Surf City is about 10 minutes north of the Route 72 Causeway on Long Beach Boulevard, sitting just past Ship Bottom at the geographic center of LBI.
Is Surf City NJ good for year round living?
Yes. Surf City has about 1,200 year-round residents and one of the tighter year-round communities on LBI thanks to its walkable downtown and concentration of small businesses that stay open through the off-season.
